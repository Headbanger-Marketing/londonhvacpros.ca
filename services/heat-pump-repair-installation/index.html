Heat Pump Installation in London, ON

London HVAC Pros, Cold-Climate Heat Pump Installation for London Homes

A heat pump heats and cools from one efficient system, and modern cold-climate models hold their own through an Ontario winter. London HVAC Pros installs heat pumps across London and the surrounding Middlesex County area, sizing each one to your home and, where it makes sense, pairing it with a backup furnace for the coldest days. It is one of the highest-impact efficiency upgrades a London home can make, and often a rebate-eligible one.

Why Homeowners Call Us

Want one efficient system for heating and cooling?

London HVAC Pros installs cold-climate heat pumps in London, Ontario for efficient year-round comfort and lower energy bills.

  • Cold-Climate Systems, Properly Sized

    We match your heat pump to your home with a load calculation and recommend a backup heat source where the climate calls for it, so you get efficient performance even on the coldest London nights.

  • One System, Lower Year-Round Bills

    A heat pump replaces separate heating and cooling equipment and runs on far less energy than older systems. We walk you through the efficiency ratings and any rebates before you decide.

  • Certified Install and Service

    Our licensed technicians install and service every major heat pump brand to spec, with clear pricing and the upkeep that keeps efficiency high and the warranty intact.
